[h1]BioEden Visual Accessibility[/h1]

This small Windows mod adds optional visual accessibility features to BioEden. It is designed for players who find the game's distant depth-of-field blur, low-contrast antenna placement preview, or colorful map difficult to read.

[h2]What it adds[/h2]
[list]
[*][b]Depth of Field toggle[/b] – turn the distant blur off in [b]Settings → Video[/b].
[*][b]Antenna Range Outline[/b] – adds a high-contrast white outline around the blue antenna range preview, including over unexplored blue areas.
[*][b]Extended Zoom[/b] – allows a little more zoom-out and smoothly moves the camera toward a more top-down view.
[*][b]Map Desaturation Filter[/b] – press F1 in the game to desaturate the map. Player buildings, minerals and polluted water remain colored so they stay easy to find.
[*][b]In-game filter button[/b] – the small F1 button appears immediately before Help in the top-right corner and follows the selected F1/F2/F3 binding.
[/list]

The filter can stay active while building and interacting. It is off after a fresh installation and can be changed at any time under [b]Settings → Accessibility[/b].

[h2]Installation[/h2]
[list=1]
[*]Close BioEden.
[*]Download the latest ZIP from the [url=https://github.com/h0n24/bioeden-visual-accessibility/releases]GitHub Releases page[/url].
[*]Extract the ZIP anywhere.
[*]Run [b]Install.cmd[/b]. If the installer cannot find your game automatically, run [b]install.ps1[/b] and provide the folder containing [b]BioEden.exe[/b].
[*]Start BioEden normally.
[/list]

The mod does not need a mod loader or the .NET SDK. The installer keeps backups of the original game files and verifies supported versions. To remove the mod, close the game and run [b]Uninstall.cmd[/b].
